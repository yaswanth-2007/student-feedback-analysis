Three Simple Steps to Evaluative Excellence

Distraction-free, honest feedback designed for students and faculty.

1. Select Your Course

Choose your active enrolled course from the institutional catalog. No account creation or student login is required.

2. Rate Key Dimensions

Evaluate instructional clarity, course materials, support, and assessment fairness on a standardized 1 to 5 scale.

3. Institutional Action

Responses are aggregated using transparent Python statistical models to generate actionable faculty and curriculum reports.
